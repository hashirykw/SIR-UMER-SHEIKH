About

Fifteen years of answers that cite their source.

Sir Umar Sheikh teaches Cambridge O Level and IGCSE Islamiyat and AS & A Level Islamic Studies in Karachi and online. His students know him for one rule: no point goes down without its reference.

The method

The Islamiyat mark scheme asks candidates to support what they say from the Qur’an and the Hadith. It is the requirement students most often leave out of an otherwise correct answer, and it is where Sir Umar’s teaching starts.

Past papers are mapped to topics so students see what Cambridge keeps returning to, and exam technique is taught alongside the content: how a 10-mark part (a) is built, and how a 4-mark part (b) earns its marks with reasoning rather than repetition.

The experience

He has taught for more than fifteen years, including at Beaconhouse and The City School. He now teaches A Level Islamic Studies at Alpha College and across the Alpha Schools Network, and online through Studere Academy and Cambridge Online. He has also taught at Averroes Institute, at Kashan’s Academy for the May/June 2024 batch, and through Signature Institute.
